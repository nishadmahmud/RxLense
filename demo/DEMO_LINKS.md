4. Embed the 6 screenshots listed above.
5. **Attachments → Project Links:** GitHub, API health, Android APK, iOS Expo update (and video if you have one).
6. Click **Submit** (top right). Drafts do not count.
7. Fill the post-submit Google Form from the competition page.
