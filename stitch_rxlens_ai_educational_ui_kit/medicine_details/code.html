category Drug Class

Loop diuretic

healing Why it is used

  • Treats fluid retention (edema) in people with congestive heart failure.
  • Treats edema caused by liver disease.
  • Treats edema caused by a kidney disorder such as nephrotic syndrome.
  • Used alone or with other medications to treat high blood pressure (hypertension).

warning Side Effects

  • Increased urination (expected effect)
  • Thirst, dry mouth
  • Muscle cramps or weakness
  • Dizziness, especially when standing up
  • Electrolyte imbalances (low potassium)

info Cautions

  • Do not take if you are unable to urinate.
  • May increase blood sugar levels. Monitor if diabetic.
  • Use caution if taking lithium or digoxin.
  • Avoid excessive sun exposure.
